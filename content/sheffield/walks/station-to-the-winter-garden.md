summary: Just off the train? This short loop takes in a brutalist landmark, a gallery and a forest under glass.
distance: About 2 km
duration: 1 to 2 hours
# Stops in order. Each is a story from this city; `note` is the
# walking direction or tip shown under that stop.
stops:
  - story: sheffield-tap
    note: Start with a drink in the station's Edwardian refreshment rooms, or save it for the end.
  - story: park-hill
    note: Head up the hill behind the station. The path is steep but short, and the view back over the city is the reward.
  - story: site-gallery
    note: Come back down past the station and cross Sheaf Street into the Cultural Industries Quarter.
  - story: millennium-gallery
    note: Walk up towards Arundel Gate. The gallery's long glass front faces the street.
  - story: winter-garden
    note: The gallery opens straight into the Winter Garden. Finish among the tree ferns.
---
A good first walk for new students, visitors, or anyone who has only ever seen Sheffield from a train window. It stays close to the station, and on a rainy day most of the second half is indoors.
