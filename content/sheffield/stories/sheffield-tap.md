Most station bars are somewhere to wait. The Sheffield Tap is somewhere to miss your train.

It sits in the station's Edwardian refreshment rooms, which were left derelict for decades before being restored and reopened as a bar in 2009. Tiles, mahogany and a long curving bar make it feel much older than its second life.

In the former dining room next door, behind glass, sits a working brewery. The beer list mixes what's brewed there with a long line of taps and bottles from around the world.

You can reach it from platform 1B or straight from the street by the station's front entrance, so you don't need a ticket.
