At the top of Meersbrook Park stands a black-and-white timber-framed house that was built around 1500. Bishops' House is thought to be the best-preserved house of its age in Sheffield, and walking into it is the closest you'll get to seeing how people here lived before the steel works arrived.

Inside you'll find carved plaster, oak panelling and rooms laid out as they might have looked in the 1600s. It's run by volunteers, so opening is limited, usually at weekends. Check before you go.

Even when it's closed, the climb is worth it. Meersbrook Park has one of the best views in the city, looking right across the valley to the city centre.
