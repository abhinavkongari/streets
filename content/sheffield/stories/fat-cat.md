Long before Kelham Island was full of flats and food halls, there was the Fat Cat. It opened in 1981 as one of Sheffield's first free houses devoted to real ale, and it helped kick off the city's reputation as one of the best beer cities in Britain.

A brewery started up behind the pub a few years later and grew into Kelham Island Brewery. Its Pale Rider went on to win national awards.

The pub itself hasn't changed much, and that's the point: small rooms, a coal fire in winter, a beer garden in summer, and a board of cask ales that changes all the time.

It's two minutes from Kelham Island Museum, so it's the obvious place to finish a visit.
