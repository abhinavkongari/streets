In February 2025 someone asked r/sheffield where to find a proper bakery, one that's about the bread and pastries rather than a coffee shop that happens to sell cake. Ninety-odd comments later, a clear shortlist had emerged. Three of them came up again when r/sheffield talked coffee in July 2026. As with any bakery, go early: the best things sell out.

### Crumb

No name came up more often. Crumb has shops in Crookes and Millhouses, and people were close to lyrical about the pastries. One said a Crumb croissant came close to the best Parisian ones, and others singled out the chocolate almond croissant, a cardamom cruffin, a crème brûlée pastry and some dangerously moreish Marmite cheese straws. For bread, try the malt sourdough. It isn't cheap, and one commenter found the croissants too dense for their taste, but it was the name people came back to again and again.

### Lovely Rita's Bakehouse

Lovely Rita's, on John Street, is a vegan bakery, and it was the very first reply in the thread. One fan said every non-vegan they've brought agrees it's some of the best baking around, and the pistachio croissant got a special mention. In July 2026 it was named again as the place for the best vegan bakes in the city.

### Bakers Yard and Depot Bakery

Two bakeries a short walk apart, near Kelham Island. Bakers Yard was praised for pastries, bread and sweet treats, and one commenter pointed out that it supplies many of the city's cafés, so you may well have eaten its baking already. The Depot Bakery bakes on Burton Road, and one fan called it simply "the answer": the best bread, plus good cakes and pastries. It also has a café in Hillsborough Park.

### Cafe Nort

Cafe Nort, in Crookes and on Chesterfield Road in Woodseats, is where several people buy their bread. It also does exceptional doughnuts and cakes, and in early February it bakes Runeberg tarts, a seasonal treat.

### I Said Bread

On Rushdale Road in Meersbrook, I Said Bread is small and local, and it sells out. Fans called it an unsung hero of the city's bakery scene, with some of the best bread in Sheffield and very good savoury pies.

### Kringle

Up in Wadsley, Kringle is a Danish bakery, and the baker is said to be Danish too. Fans rave about the Danish pastries, the pretzels and the cakes, and there are a few tables if you want to sit in. Not everyone was convinced, but it had plenty of votes.

### Turner's

For something more traditional, Turner's in Nether Edge (with a stall in the Moor Market) does made-to-order sandwiches, traybake-style cakes and farmhouse loaves. Someone who worked there for five years said they stuck to traditional recipes without additives. The Nether Edge shop keeps short hours, and the tradesmen buy up the sandwiches early.

### Tonco

On Sharrow Vale Road, Tonco was recommended for its lovely owners and a nice atmosphere. One regular said they've never been disappointed by anything they've tried.
