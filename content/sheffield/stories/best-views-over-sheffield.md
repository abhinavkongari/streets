Sheffield is built on hills, so almost everyone has a favourite spot for looking at it. When someone asked r/sheffield for the best view in the city in November 2023, the answers ranged from city parks to benches out on the western edge. Here are the ones that came up most.

### Cholera Monument

The first answer in the thread, and a favourite in an earlier hidden gems thread too. The monument stands in a small park on the hill just above the station, and the centre spreads out beneath it. One commenter who moved here for university said the monument grounds and nearby Norfolk Park kept them going through lockdown.

### Skye Edge

Two commenters picked Skye Edge, a little further east. One's tip was to stand "behind the little pigeon sheds". Another said it's the best view you can get from a public place.

### Meersbrook Park

Several people named the top of Meersbrook Park, one calling it a fantastic view of the city. It was once home to a bench from the Netflix series Afterlife. That's been gone for a while, and one commenter remembered people kept stealing its Netflix plaque. [Bishops' House](bishops-house.html) is at the top of the park if you want something to look at after the view.

### Parkwood Springs

The north side of the city gets a look-in too. Four commenters recommended Parkwood Springs, one for a different view from the usual ones. Another's advice was to go in from the Rutland Road side and follow the path until the best spot becomes obvious.

### Bole Hills

Joint most popular with Parkwood Springs. Four people named the Bole Hills in Crookes, one for the way the view mixes countryside with the houses of Stannington and Hillsborough. Another pointed out that it looks more over Stannington than the city centre.

### Carver Fields

One commenter's favourite is a bench in Carver Fields, near Den Bank, looking up the Rivelin Valley towards Moscar Top. They often see kestrels and curlews, and sometimes a badger in the fields if they go in the evening. Another commenter didn't know the name but agreed once they'd worked out where it was.

### Fulwood Lane

Three more picks are on or near Fulwood Lane, on the western edge of the city. The Sheffield Rotary Club viewpoint, near the top of Porter Clough, has a bench, and one commenter called it their favourite. Another suggested the top of Porter Clough at sunset. Further along the lane, the commenter who loves Carver Fields also likes the top of the Mayfield valley, and a second agreed it takes in more of the city.
