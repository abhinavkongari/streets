The Millennium Gallery is a long, bright building joined to the Winter Garden, and it holds two collections you won't find anywhere else.

The first is the Ruskin Collection. In the 1870s the writer John Ruskin founded a small museum in Sheffield so that the city's metalworkers could see great art, minerals and architecture up close. What he gathered, from paintings and drawings to casts and gemstones, is still here.

The second is the metalwork gallery: Sheffield's own history in cutlery, silver and steel, from tiny pocket knives to showpieces made to prove what the city's workshops could do.

The permanent collections are usually free. Touring exhibitions in the main galleries sometimes charge, so check before you go.
