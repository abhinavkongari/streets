In April 2026 someone posted a photo of their mega breakfast from the Moor Café to r/sheffield and asked whether anywhere in the city centre could beat it for the price. The replies turned into a list of the city's best greasy spoons, from the centre out to the edge of the Peak District.

### Moor Café

Start with the one that started it all. The Moor Café is on Fitzwilliam Gate, just off the Moor, and it's cheap for a city-centre breakfast. The poster's mega breakfast came with two slices of black pudding, and toast, fried bread and hash browns are extras. The café is run by two brothers who, according to the post, are two of the loveliest people you'll meet. Regulars chimed in to agree, and one called it their happy place. Another rated the artwork too. The photo also started a small argument: one purist worried that the hash brown is slowly being accepted as part of a full English, and that standards are slipping.

### Kelham Island and Neepsend

The second reply in the thread made the case that Kelham and Neepsend are a bit of a gem for old-school cafés. Its list included **The Crusty Cob** and **Full Stop Cafe**, both on Burton Road, **The Copper Kettle** on Copper Street and **The Diner** on Neepsend Lane.

The Crusty Cob got the most love; one regular called it their all-time favourite café. The same regular worried that new bus lanes nearby have made life harder for it, and nearby Percy Street is now pay and display, so it's worth walking if you can.

### Bloomers

Out on Abbeydale Road, Bloomers was recommended for its jumbo breakfast at a fair price. A second commenter backed it up and called the place a gem.

### The Hidden Gem

The Hidden Gem is on Ringinglow Road, heading out of the city towards the Peak District, so you might earn your breakfast getting there. It costs a bit more than the Moor Café, but people say it's worth it: one called its big breakfast "the best in the city by a mile".
