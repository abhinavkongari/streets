Kelham Island is a real island, made when a channel was cut from the River Don to power a mill. Today it's the heart of Sheffield's most talked-about neighbourhood, and the museum at its centre explains how the area, and the city, came to be.

The star is the River Don Engine, a 12,000-horsepower steam engine built in 1905 to roll armour plate. It's one of the most powerful working steam engines in Europe, and when it runs the whole hall shakes. Around it are the workshops, tools and stories of the people who made Sheffield steel famous.

Check the museum's website for opening days and engine run times before you go. Afterwards, the streets around it are full of independent pubs, bakeries and cafés.
