When someone asked r/sheffield for their favourite hidden gem in April 2023, the first replies were mostly jokes about keeping quiet. When someone asked again in December 2024, people were more generous. Put the two threads side by side and a handful of names come up again and again. These are the ones we could pin to a map.

### Whinfell Quarry Gardens

The most-recommended place across both threads. Several people named it in 2023, and in 2024 one commenter called it one of the city's more spectacular green spaces, "small and remote enough to avoid attention". It's a garden laid out in an old quarry off Ecclesall Road South, and we've written it up [on its own page](whinfell-quarry-gardens.html).

### The Hidden Gem café

Yes, that's its real name. The café sits back from Ringinglow Road at Bents Green and is run by Work Ltd, a Sheffield charity that works with people with learning disabilities. It came up in both threads: one commenter called it a fantastic coffee shop, and another said they couldn't recommend it enough.

### Saint Mars of the Desert

The top answer in 2024. This small brewery is on Stevenson Road in Attercliffe, east of the centre. One commenter said it makes some of the nicest beers they've had anywhere, in traditional European styles that no other British brewery really does. Another likes to walk out to it along the weirs. The taproom isn't open every day, so check before you set off.

### Lovely Rita's Bakehouse

Three people in 2024 pointed to this bakery and coffee shop, hidden away inside Stag Works on John Street. OpenStreetMap lists it as fully vegan. Another commenter suggested having a look around the rest of Stag Works while you're there.

### Café #9

A café on Nether Edge Road that puts on live music. In 2023 one commenter had a wonderful time at a gig there, with good baked snacks and a bring-your-own-drink setup. In 2024 another called it a place you only know about if you know, since it's a little off the main roads. A third pointed out it's hardly a secret any more, with several sold-out gigs most weeks.

### The Blake

A pub on Blake Street in S6 that got a round of support in 2023. One commenter called it a great pub, "just a shame about the massive hill". Blake Street is steep, so take it slowly.

### Alfred Denny Museum

Two commenters in 2024 mentioned this one. It's the University of Sheffield's zoology museum on Western Bank, named after the department's first professor of biology. According to the university, it opened in 1905, has been used for teaching for more than a hundred years, and many of its specimens have been there since the early 1900s. It isn't open like an ordinary museum, so book a place through the university's website first.

### Middle Eastern Shawarma

Two people named this London Road takeaway in 2023, one praising its amazing and cheap food. It's a good excuse to walk the length of London Road, which came up again and again in both threads.

### Already on Streets

Both threads also had love for places we've covered before: the giant redwood and the pond at the [Botanical Gardens](botanical-gardens.html), the [General Cemetery](general-cemetery.html) (which one commenter joked is full of ghosts), and [Kelham Island Museum](kelham-island-museum.html), where one person takes every visitor they have.
