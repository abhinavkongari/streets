Site Gallery has been showing new and experimental art in Sheffield since the 1970s, when it started life as a photography gallery. Today it's a bigger, brighter space on Brown Street, in the Cultural Industries Quarter, and it focuses on moving image, digital work and artists at the start of their careers.

Exhibitions change regularly, so each visit is different. It's usually free to visit, and it's a good place to see what artists in the North are making right now.

It's a five-minute walk from the station. The Showroom cinema is around the corner, so you can make an evening of it.
