Step off Surrey Street on a grey afternoon and you walk into a forest. The Winter Garden is one of the largest temperate glasshouses built in the UK in the last hundred years, and it holds thousands of plants from around the world under a roof of curved larch arches.

It opened in 2003 as part of the city's Heart of the City rebuild, and it has become Sheffield's living room. People eat lunch on the benches, students read under the tree ferns, and kids run the length of it while their parents pretend not to notice.

Entry is free, and it connects straight through to the Millennium Gallery, so on a rainy day you can spend a whole afternoon here without getting wet.

**Good to know:** it's right next to the Peace Gardens and a few minutes' walk from the station.
