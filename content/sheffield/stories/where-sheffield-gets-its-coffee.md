When someone back in Sheffield after a few years away asked r/sheffield for the city's best coffee in July 2026, nearly a hundred people answered. The same names came up again and again. Here are the ones locals recommended most, starting in the city centre and working outwards.

### 2323

If the thread had a winner, it was 2323, on Norfolk Row by St Marie's Cathedral. People called it the best flat white in town, and one regular summed it up as "best coffee in town by a mile". Expect rotating single-origin beans, coffee flights and someone behind the counter who knows a great deal about all of it. One fan called it the place to go when you want something really special.

### Mow's Coffee

Mow's, on Arundel Street, is where the adventurous beans are. Commenters mentioned a numbered espresso list that gets properly experimental, and one described a cup so fruity and floral it was barely coffee. It isn't cheap. Not everyone gets on with the service, but even the doubters rate what's in the cup, and the banana bread has its own fans.

### Frazer's

A few minutes down Arundel Street, near Matilda Street, Frazer's roasts its own beans on site, on a machine the owner built himself. Locals describe it as an unpretentious roastery with a good ethos and staff who are friendly, passionate and know their stuff.

### Husk

Husk, on Cemetery Road, drew some of the warmest comments. People praised how knowledgeable and passionate the person behind the counter is, and one regular said the cinnamon buns were the best they'd ever had. Lunch (flatbreads) isn't always on. It doesn't always keep regular hours either, so regulars check its Instagram before heading over.

### Kilnfolk

Kilnfolk, on Clough Road just south of the centre, turned up on list after list of the city's best speciality coffee, usually right next to 2323 and Mow's.

### TA

Up on Crookesmoor Road, TA roasts its beans in the shop. Fans also mention great vibes, good food and a fantastic cheese selection.

### Cloud Coffee

Cloud, on Crookes, was one of the first names in the thread. One local admitted they might be biased because it's round the corner from home, and was promptly told that Cloud is genuinely good anyway.

### Chantrey Press

Out in Woodseats, on Chesterfield Road, Chantrey Press gathered a string of votes in its own right. People describe a cosy little place with great coffee, good sweet treats and very friendly staff. One commenter found it while getting tattooed nearby and has been going back ever since.

### Smith Street Coffee Shop

Smith St roast their own beans, and several commenters named them the city's best roaster. Their café is out in Attercliffe, on Sussex Road. Commenters said the people are lovely.
