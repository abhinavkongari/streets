New to Sheffield this term and already tired of your room? You're not the first. In June 2026 someone asked r/sheffield where to study with a coffee, and in September 2026 a newcomer moving to S2 asked which cafés are happy with people working all day. These are the places locals pointed them to.

A word on manners first. Not every café wants someone on a table for hours, so keep ordering drinks. The places below are the ones locals say are happy for you to stay a while.

### Hygge

Hygge came up in both threads. The branch on Fitzalan Square has loads of plugs, a mix of seating from big communal tables to individual pods, and seats outside for warmer days. Food and coffee are decent too. The branch in Elshaw House, near Pound's Park, has a different layout that one regular found good for quiet study. They got through a good chunk of a book there, while most people on their own were plugged in with laptops. There's a third Hygge on Eyre Street.

### Lykke

On New Era Square, Lykke is a café-bar that opens late morning. It has lots of charging points and plenty of other people working. Commenters say it offers a flat price for unlimited coffee refills all day, which makes a long session much easier to justify.

### Showroom Cinema café

One commenter had heard that the café at the Showroom, the independent cinema near the station, is good for working, and another confirmed it: there's a co-working price that includes unlimited hot drinks.

### Union St

At the top of the Moor, Union St has a café downstairs and shared workspace upstairs. Freelancers use it, and co-working members get a discount in the café, so it's worth knowing about if you find you need a desk more often.

### The Refinery

On Ecclesall Road, near Waitrose, The Refinery is a charity café with great coffee, good wifi and a very welcoming feel. One regular said there's lots of space and the staff actively welcome people who want to stay and work.

### Forum Kitchen + Bar

The Forum, on Devonshire Street by Devonshire Green, mixes diners and laptops during the day. It was recommended for good food, nice coffee and a proper co-working atmosphere.

### Kilnfolk

Kilnfolk, on Clough Road, is better known for its coffee, but one commenter often reads there for an hour or so and says the staff don't seem to mind, as long as you order another drink now and then.
